Short business requirements:
Commission structure:
20% commission on first order
10% residual commission on lifetime repeat orders
Biweekly payouts once customer invoices are paid


SRS:
Feature1: Commission Plan
1. Admin can generate comission Plan
    there will be following fields:
    target_period -> date range
    sales person-> selection field [multiple sales person selection , many to many field]
    disbursment frequency -> selection field [Biweekly/Monthly/weekly]
    first_order_comission -> input field
    residual_comission -> input field

Business rules:
1. Only admin can create the comission Plan
2. There should be an approval layer : draft->approved
3. sales persion can view only his/her comission plan,who are assigned to the comission plan.

Feature 2: Comission 
1. Realtime comission will be shows based on the active comission plan. [list view]
 -> There should have two menu -> My Comission, Comission
 -> Under the My Comission menu, logged user [sales person] can see his/her comission
 -> Under the Comission menu, admin can see all comissions, sales person can't see this page.
 -> report can be group by sales_persion, disbursment_frequency 
 -> a status should be shows here comission is
