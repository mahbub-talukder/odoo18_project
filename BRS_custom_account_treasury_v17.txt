Business Requirements Specification (BRS)
Module: custom_account_treasury (Odoo 17)
Purpose: Treasury payments and receipts with multi-line distribution, tax support, document picking, and advance (anticipo) handling.

1. Scope and Users
- Extends Odoo Accounting payments (account.payment) to support:
  - Distributing one payment amount across multiple outstanding documents and other ledger items.
  - Selecting move lines and invoices to pay directly from the payment form.
  - Handling exchange difference/writeoff, taxes on payment lines, and analytic distribution.
  - Managing advances (anticipos) for customers, suppliers, and employees, including default sequences and journals.
- Users: Treasury users and managers via custom security groups. Access integrated into Accounting menus.

2. Key Concepts and Data
- Payment Header (account.payment) additions:
  - payment_line_ids: One2many of Account Payment Detail lines.
  - move_diff_ids: Track related journal entries for differences.
  - destination_account_id: computed counterpart account (receivable/payable/transfer/advance account).
  - company_currency_id, currency_id: currency handling per journal/company.
  - Document pickers: customer_invoice_ids, supplier_invoice_ids, account_move_payment_ids.
  - Domain helpers: domain_move_lines, domain_account_move_lines to filter picks.
  - Advance handling: advance (bool), advance_type_id, code_advance (sequence), partner_type extended with employee.
  - Write-off controls: writeoff_account_id, writeoff_label, payment_difference_line.
  - invoice_cash_rounding_id (standard field exposed in views).

- Payment Detail Line (account.payment.detail):
  - Represents a single accounting line to be posted with the payment.
  - Types via display_type (asset_cash, invoice, bill, reverse, entry, tax, rounding, counterpart, diff, diff_curr, advance).
  - Links to selected move line (move_line_id), related invoice (invoice_id), account_id, amounts in company and foreign currency, analytic_distribution, taxes.
  - Technical flags: is_main (header-driven lines: cash/counterpart/advance), is_counterpart, is_transfer, etc.

- Advance Types (advance.type):
  - name, account_id (receivable/payable), internal_type (related), company_id.
  - Account extension: used_for_advance_payment boolean implies reconcile True.
  - Company extension: advance_payment_journal_id default journal for advance applications.

3. Functional Requirements
3.1 Payment Creation & Editing
- User can create a payment (inbound/outbound/transfer) and add Payment Detail lines:
  - Manually via editable one2many grid.
  - Semi-automatic via search fields: pick invoices (customer/supplier) or other move lines. System creates corresponding detail lines with default amounts/currencies, taxes, analytics.
- System auto-computes main cash/bank line and counterpart/advance line(s) in payment_line_ids using _recompute_dynamic_lines_payment based on:
  - payment.amount, payment.type, selected payment_line_ids (excluding asset_cash).
  - If advance=True, create an advance main line on destination account.
  - Compute difference and select writeoff account when needed.
- Currency conversions:
  - Lines store both company currency and line currency amounts. Conversion handled on onchange of amounts, date, currency, and account-level currencies.

3.2 Taxes on Payment Detail Lines
- When a payment detail line includes taxes, the module auto-creates a matching tax line (auto_tax_line=True) with tax_repartition_line_id and tags based on tax compute_all (handle_price_include=True). Those auto lines are removed/rebuilt when inputs change.

3.3 Distribution Proposal
- Button “Proponer distribución de pagos” fills payment_line_ids by scanning pending receivables/payables by partner and company, ordered by due date, allocating the header amount until exhausted.

3.4 Counterpart Cleanup
- Button “Eliminar Lineas” clears detail lines (except needed main lines) in draft and recomputes main lines.

3.5 Destination Account Resolution
- destination_account_id computed based on:
  - Transfer: company transfer account.
  - Reconciled invoices: account from first reconciled invoice.
  - Partner type: receivable for customers, payable for suppliers.
  - Advance: advance_type.account_id overrides.
  - Employee payments: destination is change_destination_account value.

3.6 Posting (Confirm)
- Sequences:
  - If advance=True, generate code_advance from a specific sequence per partner_type.
  - For employee payments, assign name from employee payment sequence.
- Move Creation:
  - The module overrides _prepare_move_line_default_vals to replace the standard counterpart with many lines reflecting payment_line_ids (including taxes, analytics, invoice references, and cross-links to original move lines via line_pay and inv_id).
  - Liquidity move line name can reflect advance code.
- After posting, the module reconciles payment-generated lines with their referenced move lines when account and partner match and the invoice line is not reconciled.

3.7 Synchronization and Safety
- Read-only synchronization hooks ensure move/payment consistency and shared currency across move lines; partner consistency checks are documented but relaxed.
- Payment copy removes detail lines.

3.8 Rounding & Differences
- payment_difference_line aggregates payment_line_ids amounts to guide counterpart amount and potential write-off account selection (income/expense currency exchange accounts when currency differences arise).

3.9 Invoice Form – Advance Application
- On posted invoices, users can see available advance payment move lines by partner and apply them using Apply Advance button:
  - System creates a journal entry in company’s advance journal moving amounts from advance account to receivable/payable.
  - Then reconciles these new lines against the invoice and the original advance lines.
- Invoice indicates presence/count of advance payments; provides many2many listing.

3.10 Sequences and Security
- Sequences for employee payment, and advances per partner type (supplier, customer, employee).
- Security groups for treasury user and manager; menus under a “Tesoreria” section including payment lists and settings for payment methods and advance types.

4. Non-Functional / Constraints
- Works with Odoo Accounting; assumes reconcile=True on accounts used for advances.
- Posting rules: Do not delete posted journal items; all line shaping must occur prior to posting.
- Multi-company/multi-currency supported via company and currency fields; conversions use company currency and payment date.

5. UI and Views
- Payment form extended:
  - Distribution buttons, advance controls (advance, advance_type), destination/outstanding accounts, code_advance, payment difference field.
  - Notebook page “Movimientos” with a one2many editable list of payment detail lines including taxes and analytics.
  - Search pickers for invoices and move lines with domains filtered by partner/company/state/residual.
- Account move form extended with Advance tab showing linked advance move lines and an Apply Advance button.
- Menu entries under Tesoreria: receipts, payments, transfers, settings (payment methods, advance types).

6. Edge Cases and Validations
- Prevent deletion of payment term lines on move lines.
- Ensure same currency across payment move lines; raise if mixed.
- Handle both positive and negative residuals (refunds) when allocating.
- Ensure sequences exist for the selected partner-type advances; otherwise raise a user error.

7. Reporting/Navigation
- Action to open journal entries related to differences (move_diff_ids).
- Quick access to reconcile view from payment header.

8. Migration Notes (context)
- In Odoo 17, custom lines could be written to a draft move before posting. In Odoo 18, prefer shaping lines via _prepare_move_line_default_vals and avoid modifying posted moves.

End of BRS.
